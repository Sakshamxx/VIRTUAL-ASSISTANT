Data-Driven Recommendations
  • 📍

    Move CTAs to the Top of the Page

    CTA Position is the #1 feature (25.5% RF importance) and has the 3rd highest LR coefficient (+0.638). Of 500 pages, only 71 have a Top CTA. Pages with CTAs above the fold see significantly higher conversion probability. Move primary action buttons before the scroll.

    CRITICAL — QUICK WIN
  • 📝

    Improve Content Clarity on All Pages Scoring Below 3/5

    Content Clarity is the #2 feature (25.2% RF importance, +0.996 LR coefficient). 122 of 500 pages score 1/5. Rewriting with clear value propositions, bullet benefits, and plain language will directly lift the model's predicted score for those pages.

    CRITICAL PRIORITY
  • 🔘

    Add CTAs to All 255 Pages That Currently Lack One

    CTA Presence is the #3 feature (23.6% RF importance, highest LR coefficient at +1.565). This is the single biggest lever. Even adding a simple "Get in Touch" or "Apply Now" button to CTA-less pages will materially improve conversion probability.

    CRITICAL PRIORITY
  • ⚡

    Reduce Page Load Times — Especially Pages Over 1.5s

    Load_Time_ms is the #4 feature (15% RF importance, LR coefficient -0.308 — it hurts conversions). Compress images, enable lazy loading, and remove render-blocking scripts. Target sub-500ms for all landing pages.

    MEDIUM PRIORITY
  • 📋

    Completely Redesign Internship & Form Pages (Near-Zero Conversion)

    Internship pages convert at 7.8% and Form pages at 6.8% — the lowest of all types. These need a different strategy: multi-step forms, progress bars, testimonials, and clearer outcome messaging. The current design is losing almost every visitor.

    URGENT REDESIGN
  • 🎯

    Double SEO & Ad Budget on Informational (84.3%) and Service (82.6%) Pages

    These page types already convert at very high rates. They are Graphura's strongest conversion assets. Increasing qualified traffic to these pages through SEO and campaigns will give the highest return on marketing spend with no redesign needed.

    GROWTH OPPORTUNITY
Model Selection & Senior Explainer

Why We Chose These 3 Models

Logistic Regression (recommended) — Achieves the best AUC (0.87) with the smallest train-test gap (1%). It produces clear coefficients showing exactly which features help or hurt. Easy to present, easy to trust.

Decision Tree — A visual flowchart of rules. You can literally print and walk your dev team through it: "If CTA present AND clarity > 3.5 → mark page as High Conversion." Best for internal workshops.

Random Forest — 100 trees voting together. Most robust to noisy data. Best for getting reliable feature importances that tell the design team what to prioritize. Worth keeping as a secondary validator.
